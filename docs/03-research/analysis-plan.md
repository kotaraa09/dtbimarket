# Analysis plan

Written before data collection begins. Changes after data exists must be dated and justified in place — do not silently edit.

## Primary metric — 7-day action rate

For each `Recommendation` with a non-null `delivered_at`:

- **Acted** = at least one `Event` exists where `store_id` matches, the event `type` maps to the recommendation's `action_type`, and `occurred_at` is within 7 days after `delivered_at`.
- **Rate** = acted / delivered, per variant.

Excluded: `is_seed = true`; recommendations delivered within 7 days of the study end; stores with zero published products at delivery time.

## Model

Action (0/1) as the outcome, variant as the predictor, **store as a fixed effect**. Recommendations to the same store are not independent — a store that acts on one is more likely to act on the next.

## Secondary metrics

Time from delivery to action. Persistence — action rate in the final third of the study compared with the first third. Dismissal rate.

Exploratory, and reported as such.

## What will not be claimed

Revenue effects. Buyer traffic is generated by the sellers' own marketing and is outside our control, so sales are reported descriptively and no causal claim is made from them.
